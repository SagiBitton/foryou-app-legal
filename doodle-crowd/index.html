Doodle Crowd Hunt

Privacy Policy and Support

Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Data we collect

Doodle Crowd Hunt does not collect, store or sell personal data. There is no account and no sign-up. Your best level, best score and sound settings are saved only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising and tracking

The game has no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Purchases

There are no purchases in the game.

Permissions

The game does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.

Support

Questions, ideas or a problem with the game? Write to engbitton@gmail.com and we will get back to you.

How to play

A doodle crowd walks across the screen. Find the people shown at the top of the screen and tap them before the timer runs out. A wrong tap costs a heart. Stuck? Tap the light bulb for a hint.
