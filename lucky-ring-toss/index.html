Lucky Ring Toss

Privacy Policy and Support

Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Data we collect

Lucky Ring Toss does not collect, store or sell personal data. There is no account and no sign-up. Your progress, coins and collection are saved only on your device.

Advertising

The game is free. You can choose to watch a short video ad from Google AdMob to get a reward, such as extra rings or coins. Ads only play when you tap a button to watch one.

The game asks Google for non-personalized ads only and does not ask to track you across other apps. To show and measure ads, Google may still process limited data such as your IP address, approximate location, device information and how you interact with the ad. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

There are no real-money purchases. Everything in the shop is bought with in-game coins.

Permissions

The game does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.

Support

Questions, ideas or a problem with the game? Write to engbitton@gmail.com and we will get back to you.

How to play

Pull back on the ring and let go to throw it. Ring the prizes shown at the top of the screen to clear each level.
