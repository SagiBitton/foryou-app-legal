Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Your videos

When you choose a video, it is read only in your phone's memory to show the scan. It is never uploaded, never sent to a server and never saved. It is cleared as soon as you check another video or close the app.

Data we collect

ForYou App itself does not collect, store or sell personal data, and there is no sign-up.

Advertising

ForYou App is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device information and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. ForYou App never sees or stores your payment details.

Permissions

ForYou App uses your phone's standard file picker, so you choose exactly which video it can see. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location or full media library.

Children

ForYou App is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new effective date. Questions can be sent through the contact details on the app's store listing.
